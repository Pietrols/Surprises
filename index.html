Physical Maintenance, Shop Fabrication & Deal Economics

Three complete, zero-dependency, local-first applications built and cross-reviewed by Claude, Codex, and OpenCode. Click any tool below to open it directly in your browser.

01 · Industrial Maintenance (Claude + OpenCode)

Auscult

Acoustic condition screening for rotating machines from a phone microphone. Learns a machine's healthy baseline, tracks a 0–100 health index on the A–D zone strip, and diagnoses bearing defects (BPFO, BPFI, 2x BSF, FTF), imbalance, misalignment, looseness, cavitation, and electrical hum.

  • • Built-in induction motor & bearing fault simulator
  • • Live microphone + WAV/MP3/M4A recording analyzer
  • • Printable A4 client condition report & offline PWA

02 · Workshop Fabrication (Codex + OpenCode)

Second Cut

Reverse production planner for sheet offcuts. Enter the awkward pieces of plywood or MDF you already have and discover verified objects you can make right now, backed by an independent guillotine cut-tree validator and reversible inventory transactions.

  • • Shorthand offcut entry (e.g. 500x300x2, 420x180)
  • • Interactive SVG sheet-to-part layout & cut sequence
  • • Printable shop job packet & atomic build/undo

03 · Commercial Deal Economics (OpenCode)

Margin Autopsy

Deterministic Fixed-Bid & Scope-Drift X-Ray. Paste a client scope or feature list to expose the 2 or 3 deliverables driving tail-risk overruns, simulate 2,000 log-normal effort runs, test contractual Scope Surgery, and generate ready-to-paste Change-Order Shields.

  • • 4 one-click preset briefs (SaaS, Industrial ZMW, 3D Web, Clean)
  • • Honest Phase-2 revenue carving & Capped Allowances
  • • Printable Client Statement of Work Boundary Schedule
